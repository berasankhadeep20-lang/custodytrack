CustodyTrack

A PNR-linked custody record for train linen — so a passenger walking off with a blanket costs the passenger, not the coach attendant's salary.

The problem

Indian Railways coach attendants are financially liable for missing blankets, pillows, and bedsheets — the cost is deducted from their pay — even though they have no real way to stop a passenger from taking linen off the train. CustodyTrack doesn't try to prevent theft directly; it makes sure the accountability lands on the person who actually had custody of the item.

How it works

1

Issue

Attendant issues the full linen kit (blanket, pillow, 2 sheets, towel) per berth from the coach's passenger chart.

2

Acknowledge

Passenger confirms receipt via OTP or QR scan — either way, one record ties the kit to their PNR.

3

Return

Attendant marks items returned, item by item, any time before the passenger's stop.

4

Reconcile

Anything still unreturned at destination is flagged against the passenger's PNR — never the attendant.
